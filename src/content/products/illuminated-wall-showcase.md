Sized to sit between the boutique's freestanding oval cases, offering display opportunities at a smaller, wall-mounted scale. The same champagne-bronze framing and low-iron glass carry over from the central cases, so the two read as one family of fixtures rather than separate systems.

LEDs are recessed along the interior edge of the frame and shielded so the light source stays hidden — the case reads as glowing from within rather than lit from a visible fixture.
