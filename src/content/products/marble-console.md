The top and legs read as a single folded slab, but the corners are mitred at 45° and finished without a visible joint line — a detail that takes longer at the workshop than a butt joint, but reads as one continuous piece of stone once installed.

Fixing is hidden throughout: a concealed metal bracket ties the console to the wall, and an adjustable foot — hidden under the base — takes up any unevenness in the floor. Marble is available honed or polished.
