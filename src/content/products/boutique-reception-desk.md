Positioned at the transition between the store's entrance and its display floor, the desk is built from the same three materials that run through the rest of the boutique: light natural stone for the working surface, smoked oak for the body, and champagne-bronze trim along every visible edge.

The rounded front face echoes the wide, rounded transitions used between the store's other spaces, so the desk reads as part of the architecture rather than a piece of furniture placed into it.
