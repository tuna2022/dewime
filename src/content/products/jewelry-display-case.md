Developed for the boutique's central sales floor, where the case reads as a plinth for the jewelry rather than a container for it. Low-iron glass keeps colour distortion out of the case, so gemstones and metalwork read at their true tone under the controlled LED lighting recessed into the frame.

The base is finished in the same light-coloured stone used across the store's floors and walls, so the case sits within the material palette rather than interrupting it. Champagne-bronze trim runs the full perimeter, mitred at the corners with no visible fixings.
