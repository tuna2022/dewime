This shelving system started as a one-off joinery detail for a client's loft. The bracket connection was simple enough, and useful enough elsewhere, that the studio turned it into a standalone product with configurable bay widths and shelf counts.

Each shelf edge carries a concealed, low-voltage LED strip (warm white, 3000K) behind an aluminium profile, so the light line stays hidden and even rather than showing as a row of fixtures.

Every piece ships flat and assembles without visible fixings.
