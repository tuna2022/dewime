Light is thrown both up and down the wall panel it sits on, catching the ridges of the fluted surface beneath it rather than washing the wall flat. The two-way spread keeps the fixture from reading as a single hot point in the room, softening the light across the consultation and circulation areas where it's used.

The body is a single champagne-bronze casting, fixed flush to the panel with no visible screws — the mounting plate sits behind the fluting, hidden in the shadow line it creates.
