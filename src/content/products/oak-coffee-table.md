Built from reclaimed oak beams rather than milled new timber, so every plank carries its own grain, knots and old tool marks. The top is left with a natural, aged finish instead of a lacquer that would flatten the texture out.

Legs and cross-rails are joined with 12mm wooden dowels, visible at the corners rather than hidden — a construction detail borrowed from older farmhouse furniture, chosen for how it ages rather than how it looks on day one.
