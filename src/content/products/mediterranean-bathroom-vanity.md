The countertop, integrated basin and side panels are cut from a single stone specification — honed travertine or beige marble — so the sink reads as part of the slab rather than something dropped into it. Below, drawer fronts and cabinet doors are solid oak or oak veneer, left in their natural colour.

An open shelf at the base, sized for folded towels and a basket, keeps the piece from reading as a sealed box. Hardware is a single matte black or bronze knob per drawer.
