Three solid 12mm square-section steel rods are hand-formed into a single curving, interlaced form, welded to an internal anchoring plate and set into a cast concrete base — no visible fixings above the surface.

The rods are eased at every edge and ground flush at the weld lines, so the piece reads as one continuous gesture in brushed steel rather than three separate elements. The concrete base is left with a fine, undressed architectural finish, deliberately plainer than the sculpture it supports.
