Most pieces in our catalogue didn't start as products. A console built to close out a corner of a loft, a joinery detail solved once for a client and then refined, a display case designed for a single boutique — each began as a one-off answer to a specific room, and only became a standalone piece once we saw it was worth repeating.

Because the same studio designs the space and the object inside it, a piece is drawn against the material palette, proportions and light of the project it's for, then built in our own workshop rather than handed off to a catalogue supplier. Wood, stone and metal are worked by the same hands from rough material to finished joint, so a dovetail, a mitred corner or a hidden fixing can be adjusted on the bench, not just on paper.

## What's included

- Bespoke furniture, lighting and fixture design
- Material selection and finish specification
- Prototyping and full-scale mockups
- In-house fabrication and joinery
- Small-batch production for repeat pieces
- Custom fixtures developed alongside an interior or retail fit-out
