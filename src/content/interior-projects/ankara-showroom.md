The client manufactures furniture on site and needed a showroom that could be reconfigured every season without contractor involvement. We designed a demountable wall and track system that the client's own staff can rearrange in a day.

A visible workshop window lets showroom visitors watch pieces being finished, turning production into part of the retail experience.
