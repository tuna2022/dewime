Backlit oak shelving runs the full length of both side walls, so the frames themselves — not overhead fixtures — carry most of the room's light. A run of mirrored panels between the bays doubles the depth of the display without widening the floor plan.

At the centre of the room, an oval stone plinth holds the case for the collection's key pieces, angled so it reads as a single object from the entrance rather than a counter. Warm oak, pale stone and soft daylight from the rear window keep the space closer to a residential interior than a typical retail fit-out.
