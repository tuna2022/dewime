---
question: "What does an architectural design and implementation studio do?"
order: 1
---

A design and implementation (design-build) studio like Memoria Loci handles both the architectural design of a building and its physical construction, rather than handing drawings off to a separate, unrelated contractor. We take a project from concept design and permitting through to on-site construction management and handover, so the same team that designed the building is responsible for building it correctly.
