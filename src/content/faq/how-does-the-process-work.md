---
question: "How does the \"design-without-memories\" process work, and when can I receive my product?"
order: 2
---

The process consists of five stages.

In the first stage, share an image of the desired product—whether it is a painting, sculpture, piece of furniture, or a space—with us.

In the second stage, we will contact you via email or phone. We will respond within two business days with a production schedule and a price quote.

The third stage is crucial because, following the receipt of the down payment, technical details—such as detailed drawings, material selections, colors, and textures—are finalized through an online or in-person meeting.

The fourth stage involves the production process (after the quoted amount has been paid).

In the fifth stage, the product or completed space is delivered (and the remaining balance of the quoted amount is paid).
