Every project begins with the site itself — its light, its climate, its neighbours, its history. Our design process moves from concept sketches and massing studies through to fully coordinated construction drawings, structural and MEP coordination, and municipal permit submission (**ruhsat**).

We design houses, apartment buildings, offices and mixed-use developments that respond to how people actually live and work, not to a template. Every drawing set we hand to a contractor has already been tested against buildable detail, budget and the realities of construction in Türkiye.

## What's included

- Site analysis, zoning and feasibility review
- Concept design and 3D massing studies
- Design development and material specification
- Full construction documentation (uygulama projesi)
- Municipal permit and approval coordination
- Structural, mechanical and electrical engineering coordination
