Drawings only become buildings through careful site management. Our implementation teams stay on site for the full construction period, running the contractor tender, coordinating trades and checking every detail against the original design intent.

Because the same studio designs and delivers the project, decisions on site are fast and nothing gets lost in translation between architect and builder — a common failure point in projects that split design from execution.

## What's included

- Contractor tendering and selection
- On-site supervision and quality control (şantiye denetimi)
- Materials sourcing and procurement
- Schedule and budget management
- Snagging, handover and as-built documentation
